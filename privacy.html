Lyra Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Lyra (“Lyra”, “the Bot”, “we”) is a music bot for Discord. This policy explains what data Lyra collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using Lyra you agree to this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Lyra works through slash commands and collects only what is needed to run them:

  • Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, Voice Channel IDs and Role IDs.
  • Server settings configured by admins (for example volume, loop mode, DJ role, 24/7 mode).
  • Music queue and playback state for each server while the bot is active.
  • Song names, links or search queries you enter in slash commands.
  • Basic error logs (command name, error message, timestamp) for debugging.

Lyra does not read, store or process regular message content. Lyra does not use the Message Content, Server Members or Presence privileged intents. Lyra does not record, store or listen to voice conversations.

3. How We Use Data

Data is used only to provide and improve Lyra’s features: playing music, managing queues and saving server settings. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI/ML models.

4. Third-Party Services

To find and stream music, search queries or links you provide may be sent to third-party music sources and APIs. Our hosting and database provider also processes the data above on our behalf. These services have their own privacy policies. Lyra is not affiliated with Discord Inc.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on secured servers and access is limited to the developer. We use reasonable measures to protect data, but no system is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

Server settings are kept while Lyra is in your server. If Lyra is removed, that server’s data is deleted within 30 days. Error logs are kept for up to 30 days. Queue data is temporary and cleared when playback ends or the bot leaves voice.

7. Data Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of your data or your server’s data at any time by contacting us (see below). We process requests within 14 days.

8. Children

Lyra is not intended for users under the minimum age required by Discord (13, or higher in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
